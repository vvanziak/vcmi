## Game 17 (72x72, 2 levels, 4 players all AI except me; Dungeon/Shakti+Malekith, NW pocket (6,7)) - in progress at day 67
Setup: normal start (20k gold, basic town, 1 hero). All four players are poor: ~1000 gold/day; no gold mine inside my pocket (Sawmill (3,15) and Crystal Cavern (16,29) only). The AIs grow ~2-3x faster (Ingham 98k by day 55) because they hold mines in the centre.
Findings:
- `build core:grail` ("Guardian of Earth") is listed as allowed at 0 gold and the bridge answers `built:true`, but nothing is built: the grail building needs the dug-up Grail. Verify a build with `get_buildable` (state `built_today`).

- A retreat dismisses the hero (he reappears in the tavern, rehire ~2500 gold); the army is lost. Same cost as a lost fight, so fight when the odds are even.
- `threats` estimates for a hero use the lowest visible stack counts; a hero with 'Month of the Gremlin' stock showed 16k and had 46k (150 gremlins, 6 nagas, 9 genies).
- Tree of Knowledge (35,27,z0) costs 2000 gold for a level; a commander resurrection costs 1500 and is asked every turn while he is dead.
- Split stacks: the server supports `ArrangeStacks` mode 3. `swap_stacks` got an optional `count` (human tip); needs a client restart.
- The map pocket: the start region is reachable only through (28,29)->(30,34)->(35,27); the Subterranean Gate (35,36,z0)<->(35,37,z1) is guarded by Royal Griffins; z1 has a Magic Well (36,43), Sanctuary (36,40), Black Market (40,36), Rally Flag (36,33), Obelisk (30,33) and creature banks (Naga (42,31), Griffin Conservatory (43,40), Medusa Stores (27,39), Cyclops Stockpile (32,30), Dragon Fly Hive (26,36,z0), Dwarven Treasury (45,34,z0)).
- Stock: unspent weekly stock accumulates (week stock x2 after skipping), so a poor week only delays the units.
- Client crash (stock VCMI bug): `RangeGenerator::ExhaustedPossibilities - cannot place absolute obstacle` in an AI-vs-neutral battle, then `CBattleInfoEssentials::battleGetAllObstacles` throws in `CBattleAI::activeStack` -> terminate (game 17, day 3, 16:36 on 2026-10-25). All-AI games have no autosave: call `save_game` every few days.
- Game 17 result: LOST on day ~99. Blue's Ingham (242k, archangels + 269 pikemen) killed Malekith (46k) in the field and then the castle (19k + 3 arrow towers) in the same turn. Retreat/surrender were disabled in both battles.
  Root causes (human feedback, confirmed):
  1. No strategic state. I reacted to `targets` (which lists only objects already seen) and never asked "which resource has no source?". Sulfur mine was south of the castle, east of the Crystal Cavern, claimed by blue; the neutral Ore Pit (16,2), Gem Pond (29,15) and Alchemist's Lab (26,5) were a few tiles east of the castle and I found them on day 88 after being told.
  2. I read `no path` as "enclosed" when it meant fog. Exploration was done by hopping to far coordinates; step toward unexplored tiles instead.
  3. Economy chain skipped for 80 days: City Hall (+2000/day) needs Marketplace + Blacksmith + Mage Guild 1; I never built the Blacksmith. With only the Town Hall income was ~1000/day.
  4. Three armies (29k, 30k, 65k) lost to roaming heroes by walking away from the castle without checking the strongest hero in range. The AIs merge everything into one stack (Ingham 242k) and hunt the weakest hero.
  5. Gold piled up unspent (10k) while unit stock accumulated; spend gold every turn.
